`treatment` on a [visit type](/api/objects/visit-type) describes what is
included in the price: the medical examinations (`exams`) and the procedures
(`procedures`). Localized labels return only the locale keys you select with
[`Accept-Language`](/api/localization).

## Examinations

| Field | Included examination |
| --- | --- |
| `entry` | An entry examination at the start of the stay. |
| `interim` | An interim examination during the stay. |
| `final` | A final examination at the end of the stay. |

`interimFromNights` and `finalFromNights` set the stay length in nights from
which the interim or final examination is included. They are `null` when the
examination is included for every stay length, and when it is not included.

For example, `interim: true` with `interimFromNights: 14` includes an interim
examination only in stays of 14 nights or more.

## Procedures

`procedures.count` is the number of procedures included per `procedures.per`
unit: `stay`, `week`, or `day`.

`procedures.selection` says how the procedures are chosen:

| `selection` | Meaning | `items` |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hotel_offer` | Procedures are chosen from the hotel's offer. | Empty |
| `from_list` | Procedures are chosen from `items`. | Procedures to choose from |
| `fixed` | The stay includes the procedures in `items`. | Procedures with their `count` |

Each item has a `code`, a localized `label`, a `category` with its own `code`
and `label`, and a `naturalSource`. Items keep their configured order.

An item's `count` is set only for `selection: fixed`; otherwise it is `null`.
It is the number of times the procedure is included during the whole stay. It
does not follow `per`, and the item counts need not add up to
`procedures.count`.

For example, a cure of one to three weeks can include 15 procedures per week
and a fixed list:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `procedures.count` | `15` |
| `procedures.per` | `week` |
| `procedures.selection` | `fixed` |
| `items[0]` | `carbon_dioxide_bath` with `count: 6` |
| `items[1]` | `peat_wrap` with `count: 4` |
| `items[2]` | `classic_full_body_massage` with `count: 3` |
| `items[3]` | `drinking_cure` with `count: 14` |

The guest receives 15 procedures per week. The listed counts apply to the whole
stay, whether it lasts one, two, or three weeks.

## Natural sources

`naturalSource` names the natural healing source a procedure uses, or is `null`
when it uses none.

| `naturalSource` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `carbon_dioxide` | Natural carbon dioxide |
| `peat` | Peat |
| `mineral_water` | Natural mineral water |
