A price period is a range of nights in which a package has the same prices.
Use periods for a price table on a package page, for example "10 to 11 January:
from EUR 523.60 per guest for 7 nights".

Request the [visit type detail](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type)
with `expand=priceFromPeriods`. The package's `priceFromPeriods` compares all
its hotels, and each `hotelListings[]` entry has the periods of that hotel.
With `hotelId`, the detail returns only that hotel's listing, and the
package's periods still compare all hotels. The list endpoint does not return
periods.

Each period prices the package's shortest stay as if every night of the stay
had the prices and promotions of that period. Room capacity is not checked,
and the stay does not have to fit into the period. A sold-out room can set
the lowest price, and a one-night period can show a price for seven nights. When the guest chooses dates, test them
against the [stay rules](/api/visit-types#testing-a-stay-against-its-rules)
and check that a room is free.

## Which periods are returned

SpaPortal keeps a period only if a permitted stay arriving today or later
can occupy at least one of its nights. Today uses Europe/Prague. Selection
checks arrival weekdays or fixed dates, minimum and maximum stay length,
and the package's validity. Every night of that stay must have a price for
the same enabled room type and currency. A price of zero counts as a price.

For example, a season ending on Saturday is omitted when the next permitted
arrival is Sunday. If the last arrival is 20 December and the maximum stay
is 21 nights, periods starting on or after 10 January are omitted. A Christmas
period can remain because a stay starting earlier can include Christmas nights.

Selection applies separately to each hotel, room type, and currency.
The package table combines the remaining hotel periods. Periods without any
price are omitted. Room capacity does not affect selection.

Each row follows its season and promotion boundaries, without trimming
them to the next arrival or to today. A period can therefore start before
today. Its indicative amount still uses the period's rate for the shortest
stay, even if a permitted stay crossing that period has different nightly rates.

## Fields and night bounds

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `from` | First night of the period. |
| `to` | Last night of the period, inclusive. |
| `activePromotionIds` | Promotions that apply in the period to at least one room with a price, in the order of the package's `promotions`. |
| `priceFrom` | Prices in one or both currencies. Periods without a price are omitted. |

A night is identified by the date it starts on: `to: "2027-01-11"` includes
the night of 11 January, with checkout on 12 January. Periods are sorted by
date and do not overlap. Nights outside the package's validity or its price
list have no period, so the list can have gaps. Periods have no id.

## Where periods start and end

Periods come from the package's price list and validity, filtered by the
permitted stays described above. A new period starts where a season
starts or ends, or where a promotion starts or ends for a room with a price.
Two neighboring periods can therefore have the same prices, for example when
a promotion changes only the price of a more expensive room.

The package's periods combine all its hotels: a new period at one hotel also
starts a new period of the package. Each resulting period selects eligible
rooms for its own dates. A narrower package period can therefore have a
different minimum or fewer currencies than a wider hotel period, or be omitted
when no permitted stay reaches it. Display the values of the selected table.
Disabled room types are ignored: they set no prices and start no periods.

Periods also end with each [validity](/api/objects/visit-type#validity)
period of the package. With `toAppliesTo: "departure"`, the last night is
the night before `to`. With `toAppliesTo: "arrival"`, stays may continue
after `to`, up to the maximum stay length and the available price coverage.

## Price metrics

A non-null `priceFrom` has one or both currencies, `CZK` and `EUR`. Each
currency has three prices:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `perPersonPerNight` | Lowest price per guest per night. |
| `perPersonPerStay` | Price per guest for the whole stay, in the same room as `perPersonPerNight`. |
| `perStay` | Lowest price of a whole room for the whole stay. It can come from a different room or hotel. |

Every metric has these fields:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `amount` | Price after promotions, as [`Money`](/api/currencies). Display it directly. |
| `original` | Price of the same room before promotions, or `null` when promotions do not lower the rounded price. |
| `nights` | Length of the priced stay: the package's minimum or fixed number of nights, not the length of the period. |
| `occupancy` | Standard occupancy of the room the price comes from. |
| `hotel` | Hotel of that room: `id` and localized `name`. |
| `roomTypeId` | Room type the price comes from. |
| `appliedPromotionIds` | Promotions included in this price, in the order of the package's `promotions`. |

Prices assume a room at its standard occupancy and use the same nightly
prices and discounts as a booking. Overlapping promotions combine as
described in [Prices](/api/prices#promotions).

Per-person amounts are rounded separately. A room at EUR 100 per night for
three guests gives EUR 33.33 per guest per night and EUR 233.33 per guest for
seven nights. Take each amount from the API instead of multiplying rounded
values.

For a price per guest, display `perPersonPerStay.amount` with its `nights`
and `occupancy`. An occupancy of 2 assumes two guests sharing the room.
For a room price, display `perStay.amount` with its `nights` and
`occupancy`.

## Promotions in a period

Look up promotion ids in the package's [`promotions`](/api/objects/promotion).
`activePromotionIds` lists the promotions that apply in the period, and
`appliedPromotionIds` lists the promotions included in one price. A promotion
can be active without lowering the displayed price, for example when it
discounts only a more expensive room.

Show `original` struck through next to the `amount` of the same metric, and
do not apply the promotions to `amount` again.
[Building package pages](/guides/visit-types#displaying-discounts) shows how
to display a saving.

## Empty values

Packages booked on request and packages switched off in SpaPortal can still
have periods. Check `available` and `bookingMode` before offering a booking.
An ended package or a package without a future permitted stay returns an
empty array. The expanded detail also returns ended promotions; that does
not cause past periods to appear.

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Field missing | The detail was requested without `expand=priceFromPeriods`, or the response is a list. |
| `priceFromPeriods: null` | SpaPortal cannot calculate the periods for this package. Do not show a price table. |
| `priceFromPeriods: []` | No period has a price and overlaps a future permitted stay. Do not show a price table. |
