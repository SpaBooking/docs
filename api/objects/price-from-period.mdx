Request a visit type detail with `expand=priceFromPeriods` to receive
`priceFromPeriods` on the package and every `hotelListings` entry. The
expansion is independent of `expand=hotel` and is unavailable on the list.

A period describes a price-list rate. Its price uses the package's shortest
stay, as if every night had the rates and promotions of that period. It does
not check room capacity, permitted arrival days, or whether the full stay
fits inside the period. A sold-out room can provide the lowest rate, and a
one-night period can show a seven-night price. Check the guest's chosen stay
against the [stay rules](/api/visit-types#testing-a-stay-against-its-rules)
and show its room availability separately.

## Fields and night bounds

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `from` | First night of the period, inclusive. |
| `to` | Last night of the period, inclusive. |
| `activePromotionIds` | Promotions relevant to at least one eligible room in this period, in the order of the package's `promotions`. |
| `priceFrom` | Calculated rates by currency, or `null` when no room has a safely calculable price. |

Periods are ordered, do not overlap, and have no id. The date identifies the
night starting on that date: `to: "2027-01-11"` includes the night of
11 January and its checkout on 12 January.

Periods cover the intersection of configured package validity and price-list
coverage. Season changes and applicable promotion starts and ends split them
where price conditions change. A promotion affecting another eligible room
can split a period even when the cheapest room and displayed amount stay the
same. Equal amounts do not merge periods with different conditions.

Eligible rooms are active, non-draft room types at an active hotel listing,
with usable price data for that period. Disabled rooms can remain referenced
in a promotion's `appliesTo`, but do not contribute to `activePromotionIds`
or split a period.

An arrival-limited validity includes its `to` date. A departure-limited
validity ends on the night before `to`. For example, validity through
31 March produces a last night of 31 March with `toAppliesTo: "arrival"`,
or 30 March with `toAppliesTo: "departure"`. These bounds are not shifted
back by the package's minimum stay length. Overlapping or adjacent validity
intervals form continuous coverage; a gap stays a gap. No configured validity
means no date restriction.

The package's periods combine all its hotel listings. A change relevant to
one hotel can split the package's period without splitting another hotel's
period. A detail `hotelId` filter narrows only `hotelListings`; package-level
periods and promotions continue to describe all hotels.

## Price metrics

A non-null `priceFrom` has one or both currency keys, `CZK` and `EUR`. Each
currency has all three metrics:

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `perPersonPerNight` | Lowest rate per guest per night. |
| `perPersonPerStay` | Price per guest for the full stay, from the same winning room as `perPersonPerNight`. |
| `perStay` | Lowest total for the whole room at its standard occupancy. This can select a different room or hotel. |

Every metric contains:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `amount` | [`Money`](/api/currencies) after promotions. Display it directly. |
| `original` | The same winning room's amount before promotions; `null` unless its rounded original exceeds `amount`. |
| `nights` | The package's minimum or fixed stay length in nights, independent of the period's length. |
| `occupancy` | Standard occupancy of the winning room. |
| `hotel` | The winning hotel's `id` and localized `name`. |
| `roomTypeId` | The winning room type. |
| `appliedPromotionIds` | Promotions applied to this winner, in the order of the package's `promotions`; a subset of `activePromotionIds`. |

Prices use full standard occupancy and the same seasonal rates and discount
calculation as a stay. When price-list seasons overlap, their nightly rates
are added. Overlapping promotions apply successively as explained in
[Prices](/api/prices#promotions).

The per-person metrics share their winning room, hotel, nights, occupancy,
and applied promotions. Their amounts are rounded independently from the
unrounded room total. A EUR 100 nightly room rate for three guests gives
EUR 33.33 per guest per night and EUR 233.33 per guest for seven nights.
Do not multiply the rounded nightly rate to obtain the stay amount.

The per-person winner has the lowest rounded `perPersonPerStay` amount,
then the lowest rounded `perPersonPerNight` amount, then the lower occupancy,
hotel id, and room type id. Equal `perStay` totals prefer lower occupancy,
hotel id, and room type id. Per-person amounts are rounded half up from the
room total in integer minor units.

For a per-person package price, display `perPersonPerStay.amount` with its
`nights`. For a room price, display `perStay.amount` with both `nights` and
`occupancy`. Do not derive one metric from another.

## Promotions and discounted amounts

Resolve promotion ids against the package's
[`promotions`](/api/objects/promotion). `activePromotionIds` describes the
period's relevant promotions; `appliedPromotionIds` explains the particular
winning price. A promotion on another room need not reduce that minimum.

Take the discounted and original amount from the same metric. If you show
a savings percentage, calculate `100 × (1 − amountMinor / originalMinor)`
from that metric before rounding for display, and only when `original` is
non-null. Do not apply the listed promotions to `amount` again or copy one
promotion's percentage onto every price in a period.

## History and empty states

Periods include past dates within configured validity. They use the price
list and promotions currently stored, so a historical period is not a record
of a past booking's price. An expanded detail includes ended promotions so
their ids can be resolved. Ended promotions follow the same scope rules as
current promotions; they can be listed even when no returned period uses them.
Historical promotions do not add `special_offers`
to `channelContent.collections`.

`available` and `bookingMode` do not suppress period prices. Request,
disabled, and ended packages can all have priced periods. Public `validity`
contains only current and future intervals, so an ended package can have
`validity: []` and non-empty historical periods. Its current `priceFrom`
remains `null` because it has no future permitted stay.

| Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Field omitted | The detail was requested without this expansion, or this is a list response. |
| `priceFromPeriods: null` | The source price list cannot be safely interpreted. |
| `priceFromPeriods: []` | No price-list periods intersect configured validity, including when no price list exists. |
| A period with `priceFrom: null` | The period exists, but no eligible room has a calculable price. Keep its dates and promotion ids. |

A package without active hotels can retain periods with `priceFrom: null`
and empty `activePromotionIds`, while `hotelListings` is empty. A minimum
stay longer than 4,096 nights also retains periods but has no period prices.
Periods do not set availability flags; read `available` and
`unavailableReason` independently.
