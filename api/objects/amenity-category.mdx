---
title: "Amenity category"
description: "A group of related amenities for a hotel or room type."
openapi-schema: "/api/v2/openapi.yaml AmenityCategory"
---

A category groups related amenities. Use both `scope` and `code` to identify
it: codes such as `accessibility` exist for both hotels and room types.

You receive this object at `data[].category` in the
[amenity dictionary](/api/v2/reference/amenity-categories/list-the-amenity-dictionary)
and at `amenityCategories[].category` in hotel and room type responses.
Dictionary categories always include `label`. On hotels and room types,
`label` is included only with [`expand=amenityLabels`](/api/expand).

See the [amenities guide](/api/amenities) for filtering, localization, and
request and response examples.
