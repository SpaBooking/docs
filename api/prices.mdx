Hotels carry `priceFrom`: the lowest prices the hotel currently sells, computed
from its active visit types and price lists. Use it for teasers and client-side sorting,
not as a quote for concrete dates.

## Shape

`priceFrom` is keyed by currency (`CZK`, `EUR`). Each currency can contain
three independently calculated price groups:

| Key | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| `all` | Everything the hotel sells. This is the hotel-wide "from" price. |
| `health` | Only health stays (spa cures). |
| `wellness` | Only wellness stays. |

Each of them holds two minima, `perPersonPerNight` and `perStay`, with the same
fields:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `amount` | The price as a [`Money`](/api/currencies) object, after any promotion. |
| `original` | The same price before the promotion, or `null` when none applies. |
| `nights` | Shortest stay the visit type allows. |
| `occupancy` | Full standard occupancy of the room type the price applies to. |
| `visitType` | `id` and localized `name` of the visit type the price comes from. |

## All stays and the two categories

`all` looks at every visit type the hotel sells, including visit types that
have no category yet. `health` and `wellness` apply the same selection rules
to one category. In the same currency, `all.perStay.amount` is less than or
equal to `health.perStay.amount` and `wellness.perStay.amount`. The same rule
applies to `perPersonPerNight`, even when the selected offers have different
occupancies.

A category is omitted when it has no available, fully priced stay in that currency; this does
not prove that the hotel has no stays in the category. `all` is present whenever
the currency is. If a category is absent, omit its price. You can display `all`
as a clearly labeled hotel-wide price, but do not label it as a health or
wellness offer.

In the example, the Weekend stay has no category yet and the hotel sells no
wellness stay, so only `all` and `health` appear.

## Two independent minima

`perPersonPerNight.amount` is the average price per guest per night for the
selected complete stay at `occupancy` guests per room. `perStay.amount` is the
total for `occupancy` guests for `nights` nights at full standard room occupancy. Each minimum is
chosen on its own and carries its own `visitType`, `nights`, and `occupancy`,
so the two can name different visit types and room types.

Do not derive one from the other: the minima can come from different offers.
Even when they refer to the same offer, independently rounded amounts can make
`perStay.amount` differ from `perPersonPerNight.amount × occupancy × nights`.

## Selection rules

- `perPersonPerNight` is the offer with the lowest average price per guest
  per night for a complete stay.
- `perStay` is the offer with the lowest total for the whole room and stay,
  after applicable promotions. It considers all eligible room types at their
  full standard occupancy. A single, double, or larger room
  can have the lowest total.
- Each offer uses the shortest stay its visit type allows. A longer stay can
  still win when its total is lower than another offer's shorter stay.
- Ties go to the shorter stay, then the lower occupancy, then the lower
  `visitType.id`.
- Each offer corresponds to a permitted arrival date and a complete stay in
  one room type. Every occupied night must have a price in the same currency.
  The same room type must have remaining capacity for every occupied night.
  The total includes each night's applicable seasonal price.
- Arrival weekdays, specific arrival dates, and stay date restrictions apply.
  Offers without an available, fully priced, permitted stay are omitted.
- Visit types offered only on demand are excluded, even when room capacity
  is recorded.
- Promotions apply only to the nights they cover. When multiple promotions
  cover the same night, their percentage discounts apply successively.
- Visit types whose selling period has ended are ignored.
- Query parameters do not affect the values, and the cheapest offer can differ
  by currency.

## Example: single room and double room

For two available seven-night offers without a promotion:

| Room | Occupancy | Price per guest per night | Full stay total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 1 | CZK 2,000 | CZK 14,000 |
| Double | 2 | CZK 1,600 | CZK 22,400 |

`perPersonPerNight` selects the double room: `amountMinor: 160000`,
`occupancy: 2`, `nights: 7`. `perStay` selects the single room:
`amountMinor: 1400000`, `occupancy: 1`, `nights: 7`.

To display the cheapest whole package, show `perStay.amount` with its
`occupancy` and `nights`. Dividing that amount by `occupancy` gives a per-guest
share of the selected package; it does not give the lowest per-guest stay price
across all offers.

## Example: a stay across two seasons

A three-night package includes two nights at CZK 1,000 and a third night at
CZK 2,000. Its full price is CZK 4,000. A 50% promotion covering only the third
night reduces the total to CZK 3,000; `original` is CZK 4,000.

A stay contributes a price only in currencies that cover every occupied
night. The departure date does not need a nightly price.

## Availability

`priceFrom` uses the availability recorded by the API at calculation time.
Every occupied night must have remaining capacity in the same room type;
the departure date does not require capacity. Stays offered only on demand
are excluded.

For example, if a CZK 3,000 stay is sold out and the cheapest available stay
costs CZK 4,500, `perStay.amount` reports CZK 4,500.

A category or currency without an available, fully priced stay is omitted.
If no such stay exists for the hotel, `priceFrom` is `null`. This does not
rule out stays that require an availability request.

The response does not hold a room. Availability can change after the price
is calculated. Select your dates and guests to confirm availability and the
final price through the booking flow. If you cache hotel responses, refresh
them regularly; a cached price is not a room reservation.

## Rounding

The API returns full precision in minor units. Rounding to whole currency units
for display is up to you.

## Payload size

Every minimum embeds the visit type name in the requested locales. Send
`Accept-Language` to receive one locale instead of all four. A missing
translation falls back to Czech, like the hotel `name`; see
[Localization](/api/localization).

## Visit type prices

<Warning>
  **Draft:** The visit type endpoints are still in progress. Their prices may
  change before they are finalized.
</Warning>

A [visit type](/api/objects/visit-type) carries its own `priceFrom`, built with
the same selection rules as the hotel price, but for one visit type across its
hotels:

- It is keyed by currency and holds `perPersonPerNight` and `perStay` directly,
  without the `all`, `health`, and `wellness` groups.
- Each minimum names the hotel it comes from in `hotel` (`id` and localized
  `name`) instead of `visitType`. The minima of one currency can come from
  different hotels.
- Each offer uses the visit type's shortest permitted stay.
- Ties go to the shorter stay, then the lower occupancy, then the lower
  `hotel.id`.
- `priceFrom` is `null` for visit types booked on request, for unavailable
  visit types, and when no complete, permitted stay with free capacity exists.
