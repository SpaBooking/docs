Use indicative prices for teasers and comparisons. They are calculated from
complete purchasable stays and do not reserve a room or quote a chosen set of
guests and dates. The [Price from object](/api/objects/price-from) defines the
fields and currency containers.

Hotel prices are released. Package, package-at-hotel, and seasonal prices,
and the `hotel` reference in hotel minima, are not yet released on the
production API. See [API coverage](/api/versioning#api-coverage).

## Two independent minima

`perPersonPerNight` selects the lowest average price per guest per night for
a complete stay. `perStay` independently selects the lowest total for the
whole room and stay. Each uses the room type's full standard occupancy.

Do not derive one minimum from the other: they can select different offers.
Even for the same offer, independently rounded values can make
`perStay.amount` differ from `perPersonPerNight.amount × occupancy × nights`.

For two available seven-night offers without a promotion:

| Room | Occupancy | Price per guest per night | Full stay total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 1 | CZK 2,000 | CZK 14,000 |
| Double | 2 | CZK 1,600 | CZK 22,400 |

`perPersonPerNight` selects the double room. `perStay` selects the single
room. To display the cheapest whole package, use `perStay.amount` with its
reported occupancy and nights. Dividing the double-room total by two does
not produce a purchasable single-room offer.

## Selecting a complete stay

An offer is eligible only when all of these conditions hold:

- Its arrival and checkout satisfy the applicable stay rules.
- Every occupied night has a price in the same currency and the same room
  type has remaining capacity for every night.
- The visit type is offered now and permits an immediately confirmed booking.
  Stays booked on **request** are excluded even when room capacity exists.
- Its visit type, hotel, and room are eligible within the price calculation's
  scope.

Each candidate uses the shortest stay its visit type permits. When comparing
several visit types, a longer minimum stay can still win if its total is
lower. Different currencies can select different offers.

The total uses each occupied night's seasonal rate. The checkout date needs
neither a nightly price nor room capacity. A gap in prices or capacity makes
that complete stay ineligible; the calculation does not combine rooms or
currencies to fill the gap.

Equal hotel prices prefer the shorter stay, then lower occupancy, then the
lower visit type ID. Remaining ties use stable offer identities. Query
parameters on hotel catalog endpoints do not change their indicative prices.

## Comparing hotel groups

The hotel-wide `all` group compares all eligible offers. The `health` and
`wellness` groups compare offers from one category.

All three groups compare packages from the
[visit type catalog](/api/visit-types#publication-and-availability), so every
referenced package can be opened with the same public key.

For the same currency and minimum, the hotel-wide amount cannot exceed the
amount in a category group. Compare the two `perStay` values or the two
`perPersonPerNight` values; comparing across those metrics is not meaningful.

An omitted category price does not prove the hotel has no packages in that
category. Do not present a hotel-wide price as a category-specific offer.
The [container definitions](/api/objects/price-from#price-containers) specify
which groups and currencies are omitted when no offer exists.

## Promotions

Promotion eligibility follows the
[authenticated sales context](/api/authentication#channels-and-scope) and the
promotion's hotel, room, and night restrictions. Its public field definitions
and a room-limited example are on [Promotion](/api/objects/promotion).

Apply overlapping percentage discounts one after another to the nights they
both cover. For a base price of 1,000, a 10% discount followed by 15% gives
`1,000 × 0.90 × 0.85 = 765`: a combined reduction of 23.5%, not 25%.

For example, a hotel promotion shared with the hotel's own website and
SpaBooking combines with a separate SpaBooking-only promotion. The website
uses the shared 10% discount and reports 900; SpaBooking uses both discounts
and reports 765. A promotion assigned only to another channel is not
inherited. Each promotion counts once, even when it has more than one
applicable assignment.

A promotion changes only the nights within its inclusive public date range.
The original amount belongs to the **same selected offer** before those
promotions; it is not a separately selected cheapest undiscounted stay.

For example, a three-night package has two nights at CZK 1,000 and one at
CZK 2,000. A 50% promotion covering only the third night gives a total of
CZK 3,000 and an original total of CZK 4,000. Do not discount the first two
nights or extend the promotion across the whole stay.

## Visit type and hotel-pair prices

The visit type's overall price compares eligible offers across all its
eligible hotels. Each hotel entry's price limits the comparison to that
hotel and visit type. Both use the selection rules above and the same
[shared minimum](/api/objects/price-from).

Restricting the detail response's hotel entries does not change the
package-wide summary or its overall price. The
[detail endpoint](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type) defines how
to request one hotel's entries and optional expansions.

## Seasonal prices

Each seasonal price belongs to one hotel and one visit type. A season is a
configured price-list period; it is not a calendar month. Its price compares
stays whose **arrival** is in that period. Only current and future seasons
are returned. A seasonal minimum is not a quote for every arrival in that
period; prices and promotions can differ within it.

For a current season, search starts at the later of its first date and today
in Europe/Prague. A cheaper offer available only for a past arrival does not
contribute. The season keeps its original date bounds in the response.

A stay can continue into another price-list season. Price every occupied
night using its own applicable seasonal rate, while keeping the offer in
the season of its arrival. Promotions and remaining room capacity apply to
the entire candidate stay in the same way as above.

A known season without an eligible complete stay remains visible with a
null price. This also applies when the package is unavailable or booked on
request. Clients can distinguish a known period without an offer from an
absent period. Requesting seasons does not alter the package's overall
price or its hotel-pair prices.

## Availability

The price calculation checks the recorded capacity at calculation time. If
the cheapest stay is sold out, it selects the next eligible stay. For
example, a sold-out CZK 3,000 offer does not beat an available CZK 4,500 offer.

The response does not hold a room. Prices and recorded capacity can change,
so refresh cached offers regularly. An absent indicative price does not
mean the package is unavailable; check its catalog availability separately.

## Payer and the quoted amount

For a health stay, [`payer`](/api/objects/visit-type#category-payer-and-booking-mode)
describes which costs the guest pays. For `guest_and_insurer`, the quoted
price covers the guest-payable accommodation and meals. Display that amount
directly; do not subtract an insurer contribution.

## Display and rounding

Amounts use integer minor currency units. Convert them using the reported
currency exponent; rounding to whole units for display is a client choice.
The rules for the original-price field and its separately rounded average
are on [Price from](/api/objects/price-from#minimum-fields).
