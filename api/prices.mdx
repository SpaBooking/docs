Use indicative prices for teasers and comparisons. `priceFrom` selects a
complete stay with capacity; `priceFromPeriods` shows price-list rates over
time. Neither reserves a room or quotes a chosen set of guests and dates.
The [Price from object](/api/objects/price-from) and
[Price from period object](/api/objects/price-from-period) define their fields.

## Two independent minima

`perPersonPerNight` selects the lowest average price per guest per night for
a complete stay. `perStay` independently selects the lowest total for the
whole room and stay. Each uses the room type's full standard occupancy.

Do not derive one minimum from the other: they can select different offers.
Even for the same offer, independently rounded values can make
`perStay.amount` differ from `perPersonPerNight.amount × occupancy × nights`.

For two available seven-night offers without a promotion:

| Room | Occupancy | Price per guest per night | Full stay total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 1 | CZK 2,000 | CZK 14,000 |
| Double | 2 | CZK 1,600 | CZK 22,400 |

`perPersonPerNight` selects the double room. `perStay` selects the single
room. To display the cheapest whole package, use `perStay.amount` with its
reported occupancy and nights. Dividing the double-room total by two does
not produce a buyable single-room offer.

## Selecting a complete stay

A complete stay can become a `priceFrom` minimum only when all of these
conditions hold:

- Its arrival and checkout satisfy the applicable stay rules.
- Every occupied night has a price in the same currency and the same room
  type has remaining capacity for every night.
- The package is in the
  [visit type catalog](/api/visit-types#publication-and-availability) of your
  public key. Hotel prices additionally require `available: true` and
  `bookingMode: "instant"`. Visit type prices do not use these two gates.
- Its hotel, package, and room type belong to the scope of the calculation:
  the hotel's packages for a hotel price, the package's hotels for a package
  price.

Each candidate uses the shortest stay its package permits. A package with a
longer minimum stay can still win: `perStay` compares totals, and
`perPersonPerNight` compares averages per guest per night. Different
currencies can select different offers.

Package and hotel minima compare every arrival from today in Europe/Prague
onward.

The total uses each occupied night's seasonal rate. The checkout date needs
neither a nightly price nor room capacity. A gap in prices or capacity makes
that complete stay ineligible; the calculation does not combine rooms or
currencies to fill the gap.

Equal amounts prefer the shorter stay, then the lower occupancy, then the
lower `visitType.id`, hotel id, and room type id, then the earlier arrival.
Query parameters do not change indicative prices.

## Comparing hotel groups

The hotel-wide `all` group compares all buyable offers. The `health` and
`wellness` groups compare offers from one category. Every package that a
hotel price names can be opened with the same public key.

For the same currency and minimum, the hotel-wide amount cannot exceed the
amount in a category group. Compare the two `perStay` values or the two
`perPersonPerNight` values; comparing across those metrics is not meaningful.

An omitted category price does not prove the hotel has no packages in that
category. Do not present a hotel-wide price as a category-specific offer.
The [container definitions](/api/objects/price-from#where-minima-appear) specify
which groups and currencies are omitted when no offer exists.

## Promotions

Whether a promotion applies follows
[the channel of your public key](/api/authentication#channels-and-scope) and
the promotion's hotel, room, and night restrictions.
[Promotion](/api/objects/promotion) defines the fields and shows an example
limited to two room types.

Overlapping percentage discounts apply one after another to the nights they
both cover. For a base price of 1,000, a 10% discount followed by 15% gives
`1,000 × 0.90 × 0.85 = 765`: a combined reduction of 23.5%, not 25%.

For example, a hotel promotion shared with the hotel's own website and
SpaBooking combines with a separate SpaBooking-only promotion. The website
uses the shared 10% discount and reports 900; SpaBooking uses both discounts
and reports 765. A promotion assigned only to another channel does not apply.
A promotion discounts each night at most once, even when more than one of its
assignments applies.

A promotion changes only the nights from its `from` night to its `to` night.
The original amount belongs to the same selected offer before those
promotions; it is not a separately selected cheapest undiscounted stay.

For example, a three-night package has two nights at CZK 1,000 and one at
CZK 2,000. A 50% promotion covering only the third night gives a total of
CZK 3,000 and an original total of CZK 4,000. The first two nights are not
discounted.

## Package and package-at-hotel prices

The package's `priceFrom` compares complete stays across all hotels that sell
it. Each `hotelListings[]` entry's `priceFrom` limits the comparison to that hotel. Both
use the selection rules above and the same
[Price from](/api/objects/price-from) object.

Requesting one hotel with `hotelId` on the
[detail endpoint](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type) does not
change the package's `priceFrom`.

## Price periods

Request `GET /api/v2/visit-types/{visitTypeId}?expand=priceFromPeriods` to
receive the package's `priceFromPeriods` and each hotel listing's periods.
These describe the intersection of configured package validity, price-list
coverage, and applicable promotion windows, including history. Promotions
split periods where their conditions change.

Each period prices the package's shortest stay as if all its nights used
that period's rates and promotions. It does not search for an actual stay,
check room capacity or arrival weekdays, or require the full stay to fit
inside the period. A one-night period can therefore show a seven-night price.
The [period object](/api/objects/price-from-period) defines the three metrics,
night bounds, validity limits, promotion ids, and empty states.

Display the returned amounts directly, without applying promotions again.
Period rates and `priceFrom` answer different questions; their minima need
not match. Expanding periods does not change `priceFrom`.

## Room capacity

`priceFrom` is calculated for each request from the room capacity recorded in
SpaPortal at that moment. If the cheapest stay is sold out, the calculation
selects the next buyable stay. For
example, a sold-out CZK 3,000 offer does not beat an available CZK 4,500 offer.

`priceFromPeriods` ignores room capacity: its cheapest room can be sold out.
Show the actual availability when the guest chooses dates.

The response does not hold a room. A price you cache can be outdated by the
next request, because prices and recorded capacity change. An absent
indicative price does not
mean the package is unavailable; check its catalog availability separately.

## Payer and the quoted amount

For a health stay, [`payer`](/api/objects/visit-type#category-payer-and-booking-mode)
describes which costs the guest pays. For `guest_and_insurer`, the indicative
price covers the guest-payable accommodation and meals. Display that amount
directly; do not subtract an insurer contribution.

## Display and rounding

Amounts use integer minor currency units. Convert them using the reported
currency exponent; rounding to whole units for display is a client choice.
The rules for the original-price field and its separately rounded average
are on [Price from](/api/objects/price-from#minimum-fields).
