Use indicative prices for teasers and comparisons. `priceFrom` is the lowest
price of a permitted complete stay from today onward, without checking room
capacity.
`priceFromPeriods` shows how a package's prices change over time. Neither
reserves a room or quotes a chosen set of guests and dates. The
[Price from object](/api/objects/price-from) and
[Price period object](/api/objects/price-from-period) define their fields.

## Three independent minima

`perPersonPerNight` selects the lowest average price per guest per night for
a complete stay. `perPersonPerStay` independently selects the lowest price
per guest for the whole stay. `perStay` selects the lowest total for the
whole room and stay. Each uses the room type's full standard occupancy.

Do not derive one minimum from another: they can select different offers.
Even for the same offer, independently rounded values can make
`perStay.amount` differ from `perPersonPerNight.amount × occupancy × nights`.

For two priced seven-night offers without a promotion:

| Room | Occupancy | Price per guest per night | Full stay total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 1 | CZK 2,000 | CZK 14,000 |
| Double | 2 | CZK 1,600 | CZK 22,400 |

`perPersonPerStay` selects the double room at CZK 11,200 per guest for
7 nights and reports `occupancy: 2`. `perPersonPerNight` also selects the
double room. `perStay` selects the single room at CZK 14,000 for the whole
stay. Display the price per guest together with the occupancy it assumes.

Across packages, the nightly minimum can belong to a longer stay. A
21-night package at CZK 1,000 per guest per night costs CZK 21,000 per guest;
the seven-night double-room offer above is cheaper per guest for the whole
stay. Take `perPersonPerStay` directly instead of multiplying the nightly
minimum or dividing the whole-room minimum.

## Selecting a complete stay

A complete stay can become a `priceFrom` minimum only when all of these
conditions hold:

- Its arrival and checkout satisfy the applicable stay rules.
- Every occupied night has a price in the same currency for the same enabled,
  non-draft room type. Free rooms are not required.
- The package is in the
  [visit type catalog](/api/visit-types#publication-and-availability) of your
  public key. Hotel prices include only packages with `available: true` and
  `bookingMode: "instant"`. Visit type prices also include packages booked
  on request and packages switched off in SpaPortal.
- Its hotel, package, and room type belong to the scope of the calculation:
  the hotel's packages for a hotel price, the package's hotels for a package
  price.

Each candidate uses the shortest stay its package permits. A package with a
longer minimum stay can still win: `perStay` compares room totals,
`perPersonPerStay` compares per-guest totals, and
`perPersonPerNight` compares averages per guest per night. Different
currencies can select different offers.

Package and hotel minima compare every arrival from today in Europe/Prague
onward.

The total uses each occupied night's seasonal rate. The checkout date needs
no nightly price. A gap in prices makes that complete stay ineligible; the
calculation does not combine rooms or currencies to fill the gap.

Equal amounts prefer the shorter stay, then the lower occupancy, then the
lower `visitType.id`, hotel id, and room type id, then the earlier arrival.
Query parameters do not change indicative prices.

## Comparing hotel groups

The hotel-wide `all` group compares all eligible priced stays. The `health` and
`wellness` groups compare offers from one category. Every package that a
hotel price names can be opened with the same public key.

For the same currency and minimum, the hotel-wide amount cannot exceed the
amount in a category group. Compare the same metric in both groups; for
example, compare their `perPersonPerStay` values when showing per-guest
stay prices.

An omitted category price does not prove the hotel has no packages in that
category. Do not present a hotel-wide price as a category-specific offer.
The [container definitions](/api/objects/price-from#where-minima-appear) specify
which groups and currencies are omitted when no offer exists.

## Promotions

Whether a promotion applies follows
[the channel of your public key](/api/authentication#channels-and-scope) and
the promotion's hotel, room, and night restrictions.
[Promotion](/api/objects/promotion) defines the fields and shows an example
limited to two room types.

Overlapping percentage discounts apply one after another to the nights they
both cover. For a base price of 1,000, a 10% discount followed by 15% gives
`1,000 × 0.90 × 0.85 = 765`: a combined reduction of 23.5%, not 25%.

For example, a hotel promotion shared with the hotel's own website and
SpaBooking combines with a separate SpaBooking-only promotion. The website
uses the shared 10% discount and reports 900; SpaBooking uses both discounts
and reports 765. A promotion assigned only to another channel does not apply.
A promotion discounts each night at most once, even when more than one of its
assignments applies.

A promotion changes only the nights from its `from` night to its `to` night.
The original amount belongs to the same selected offer before those
promotions; it is not a separately selected cheapest undiscounted stay.

For example, a three-night package has two nights at CZK 1,000 and one at
CZK 2,000. A 50% promotion covering only the third night gives a total of
CZK 3,000 and an original total of CZK 4,000. The first two nights are not
discounted.

## Package and package-at-hotel prices

The package's `priceFrom` compares complete stays across all hotels that sell
it. The `priceFrom` of each `hotelListings[]` entry compares only that hotel.
Both use the selection rules above and the same
[Price from](/api/objects/price-from) object.

Requesting one hotel with `hotelId` on the
[detail endpoint](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type) does not
change the package's `priceFrom`.

## Price periods

Request the detail with `expand=priceFromPeriods` to receive
[price periods](/api/objects/price-from-period): ranges of nights with the
package's prices. Only periods overlapping a future permitted stay with prices
for every night remain. The [period selection rules](/api/objects/price-from-period#which-periods-are-returned)
account for arrivals, stay length, validity, and price coverage. Neither
indicative price checks room capacity.

Period amounts can still differ from `priceFrom`: periods price every night
of the shortest stay at the period's rate, even when
the stay is longer than the period. `priceFrom` uses permitted arrivals from
today and the actual rate and promotions of each night. Requesting periods
does not change `priceFrom`.

For example, a seven-night stay with its only permitted arrival has two
nights at EUR 50 per guest and five at EUR 100. Its `perPersonPerStay` is
EUR 600. The cheaper period still shows EUR 350 for seven nights at its
EUR 50 rate. A listing's minimum can therefore be higher than its cheapest
period row. Use the listing's `priceFrom` for cards and modal footers; use
its `priceFromPeriods` for the price table.

## Room capacity

`priceFrom` and `priceFromPeriods` do not check room capacity. A sold-out
CZK 3,000 stay can set `priceFrom` even when another stay with free rooms
costs CZK 4,500. Reservations and capacity updates alone do not change
indicative prices.

Check room availability in the calendar and obtain a booking quote for the
guest's chosen dates and party. An indicative price does not reserve a room
or guarantee availability.

Prices are calculated for each request. Cached values can change when rates,
promotions, package rules, or the business date change. An absent indicative
price does not mean the package is unavailable; check catalog availability
separately.

## Payer and the quoted amount

For a health stay, [`payer`](/api/objects/visit-type#category-payer-and-booking-mode)
describes which costs the guest pays. For `guest_and_insurer`, the indicative
price covers the guest-payable accommodation and meals. Display that amount
directly; do not subtract an insurer contribution.

## Display and rounding

Amounts use integer minor currency units. Convert them using the reported
currency exponent; rounding to whole units for display is a client choice.
The rules for the original-price field and its separately rounded average
are on [Price from](/api/objects/price-from#minimum-fields).
