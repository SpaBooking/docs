A [visit type](/api/objects/visit-type) is a stay package sold by one or more
hotels. Its catalog availability, its permitted stays, and its indicative
prices answer three different questions.
[Building package pages](/guides/visit-types) walks through a typical
integration.

## Publication and availability

The catalog follows the [channel and companies](/api/authentication#channels-and-scope)
of your public key. It contains only packages whose required configuration is
complete. An incomplete package is omitted from the list, and its detail
returns `404` like an unknown or inaccessible package.

A published package can still be unavailable. `unavailableReason` gives the
first applicable reason from the
[availability table](/api/objects/visit-type#availability). Availability is
evaluated for today's date in Europe/Prague and can change at midnight. The
list returns available packages by default; the detail also returns an
unavailable package.

Availability does not promise a price or a free room on a particular date. A
permitted arrival with no free room still satisfies the catalog date filters.
[Prices](/api/prices) require a complete, priced stay with room capacity.

## Testing a stay against its rules

A stay with arrival `a`, `nights` nights, and checkout `c` is permitted when:

1. `nights` fits [`stayLength`](/api/objects/visit-type#stay-length-and-arrival).
   A week-based package requires a whole number of seven-night weeks.
2. `a` satisfies [`arrival`](/api/objects/visit-type#stay-length-and-arrival):
   it falls on a permitted weekday, or it is one of the permitted dates.
3. When [`validity`](/api/objects/visit-type#validity) is an array, at least
   one period contains the whole stay: `a` lies within the period, and for a
   departure-limited period `c` is on or before `to`.

Overlapping periods are alternatives: one matching period is enough, and the
limits of two periods never combine. A `validity` of `null` imposes no date
restriction; `[]` permits no stay.

For example, the period `2026-11-01` to `2027-03-31` with
`toAppliesTo: "departure"` permits a stay that checks out on 31 March 2027.
A stay arriving on 30 March and checking out on 6 April does not fit it, even
when an April period exists. An overlapping arrival-limited period that
contains 30 March would permit that stay.

The list filters `nights`, `arrivalFrom`, and `arrivalTo` apply these rules.
Without an arrival window, `nights` checks only the stay length. With a
window, the package must permit a stay that arrives inside the window from
today onward, of `nights` nights or of its shortest length when `nights` is
omitted. The filters check neither prices nor room capacity, and they do not
recalculate `priceFrom` for the requested stay: a package matching
`nights=14` can still report a seven-night minimum.

## Hotels and prices

The hotel entries say where the package is sold. An entry stays present when
its price is `null`. The package's `priceFrom` compares offers across all its
hotels; an entry's `priceFrom` compares offers at that hotel only. Both use
the [same rules](/api/prices) and the [Price from](/api/objects/price-from)
shape.

Seasons show how the price of the package at one hotel changes with the
arrival date. They are price-list periods, not calendar months and not a
calendar of bookable dates. See [Seasonal prices](/api/prices#seasonal-prices).
