A [visit type](/api/objects/visit-type) describes a stay package sold by one or
more hotels. Its catalog availability, permitted stay dates, and indicative
prices answer different questions. Room availability and the final booking
price are checked in the [booking flow](/guides/booking).

## Publication and availability

The catalog follows the [authenticated scope](/api/authentication#channels-and-scope).
A visit type must have a Czech name and complete, valid required configuration
to be published. The API does not invent a category, payer, arrival rule, or
stay length for an incomplete record. Such a record is omitted from the list
and returns the same `404` as an unknown or inaccessible record.

A published visit type can still be unavailable. The first applicable reason
in the [availability code table](/api/objects/visit-type#availability) determines
its `unavailableReason`: disabled, ended, no permitted future arrival, then no
active hotel. Availability is evaluated using today's calendar date in
Europe/Prague and can change at midnight.

The list returns available visit types by default; the detail can return an
unavailable visit type. The endpoint references define the
[list filters](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/list-visit-types) and
[detail response](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type).

Catalog availability does not promise a price or a room on any particular
date. A permitted arrival with no free room still satisfies a catalog date
filter. [Prices](/api/prices) require a complete priced stay with capacity and
are recalculated against the sales context.

## Testing a stay against its rules

Use the field definitions for [stay length and arrival](/api/objects/visit-type#stay-length-and-arrival)
and [validity](/api/objects/visit-type#validity) when evaluating a proposed stay.

1. Check the number of nights against the configured unit and limits. A week
   stay requires a whole number of seven-night weeks.
2. Check the arrival against the selected arrival variant. Weekday rules and
   explicit-date rules are alternatives; there is no implicit Sunday rule.
3. When validity periods exist, at least one period must permit the complete
   stay. Its arrival must be within that period. A departure-limited period
   must also permit the checkout day.

Overlapping periods are alternatives: one matching period is sufficient. Do
not take the arrival from one period and a later departure limit from another.
If no validity periods are configured, the arrival rule and stay-length
limits still apply.

For example, a departure-limited period ending on 31 January allows checkout
on 31 January. A stay arriving on 30 January and checking out on 1 February
does not fit it, even when a separate March period exists. An overlapping
arrival-limited period that independently permits 30 January can allow that
same stay.

The catalog date-window filter tests for at least one permitted arrival and
stay in the requested window. It applies these rules to the requested length,
or the package's shortest permitted stay when no length is supplied. It does
not query room capacity or room prices. Request limits and parameter pairing
are defined on the [list endpoint](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/list-visit-types).

## Hotels and prices

The hotel entries identify where the visit type is sold. An entry remains
present even when its price is `null`; the relationship to a hotel is separate
from a currently purchasable offer.

The visit type's overall price compares eligible offers across its hotels.
An entry's price compares offers only for that hotel and visit type. Both use
the [same selection and discount rules](/api/prices); their field shape is
defined by [Price from](/api/objects/price-from).

Seasons show how this pair's prices change with the arrival date. They are
price-list periods, not calendar months and not a calendar of bookable dates.
See [seasonal price selection](/api/prices#seasonal-prices) and the
[hotel entry fields](/api/objects/visit-type#hotels).
