This guide walks through a package listing and a package detail with a hotel
choice and price periods. Use the same [public key](/api/authentication)
for all requests of a page and request the [languages](/api/localization)
the page displays.

## Package cards

Load the [package list](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/list-visit-types) and
follow its [pagination](/api/pagination). A card typically shows the name,
`shortDescription`, the first image, the category, the board, and the stay
length from the [Visit type](/api/objects/visit-type) object. Use
`channelContent.collections` to group the cards of your own website.

For a "from" price, show `priceFrom.{currency}.perStay.amount` with the
`nights` and `occupancy` of that minimum, for example "from CZK 12,600 for
7 nights, 1 guest". Keep every displayed value from the same minimum; the
`perPersonPerNight` minimum can be a different offer. When the minimum has a
non-null `original`, show it struck through.

When `priceFrom` is `null`, show the card without a price. Always label a
`request` package as available on request, including when it has a price.
The list returns only available packages unless you set `available`; a
package detail can be unavailable, so check `available` separately from price.

## Hotel choice

Load the [package detail](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type)
with `expand=hotel`. Each `hotelListings[]` entry carries the hotel summary
and the price of this package at that hotel. An entry without a qualifying
complete stay has `priceFrom: null` but remains a hotel that sells the
package. Fetch the [Hotel](/api/objects/hotel) detail for the full profile
and room catalog.

When the website visitor chooses a hotel, show that listing's price. The
package's top-level `priceFrom` compares all hotels and can belong to a
different hotel than the selected one.

## Stay rules and board

Present `stayLength`, `arrival`, and `validity` as the booking constraints,
and `board.meals` as what is included and what the guest can change. The
[stay-rule algorithm](/api/visit-types#testing-a-stay-against-its-rules)
tells you whether a chosen arrival and length is permitted. Check whether a
room is free when the guest chooses dates.

## Price periods

Request the detail with `expand=hotel,priceFromPeriods&hotelId={hotelId}`.
Use `hotelListings[0].priceFromPeriods` for the selected hotel's table. The
root `priceFromPeriods` continues to compare all hotels even with `hotelId`.

Keep one row per returned [price period](/api/objects/price-from-period).
Its `from` and `to` are inclusive night dates. Rates change at season and
relevant promotion boundaries inside configured package validity. Keep rows
with `priceFrom: null` as periods without a calculated price. A null periods
field means the price source cannot be safely interpreted; an empty array
means there are no periods in the configured coverage.

Periods include history. For a current-offer page, omit periods whose `to`
is before today in Europe/Prague. Do not reconstruct periods from public
`validity`, which omits ended intervals.

For a heading such as "per person, 7 nights", use
`priceFrom.{currency}.perPersonPerStay.amount` and its `nights`. For a nightly
heading, use `perPersonPerNight.amount`. For a whole-room total, use
`perStay.amount` and display both `nights` and `occupancy`. Take each value
directly from the API: multiplying the rounded nightly amount can produce
a different result.

A row is a price-list rate for the package's shortest stay. It can be priced
even when its cheapest room is sold out or no permitted full stay fits
inside that period. Present the package's rules alongside the table and show
actual room availability when the guest selects dates.

## Regular and discounted price

Take `amount` and `original` from the same metric in the same period.
`original` describes that winning room before promotions. When it is null,
show the amount without a struck-through price.

In the detail reference's `expand-hotel-price-periods` example, the package's
period from 5 to 9 January 2027 has `perPersonPerStay.amount` of EUR 523.60
and `original` of EUR 770.00 for seven nights at Hotel Bellevue. Its
`perPersonPerNight.amount` is EUR 74.80. The independently selected
`perStay.amount` is EUR 714.00, discounted from EUR 840.00, for a single room
at Hotel Pramen. Use the hotel and occupancy belonging to the metric you
display; the cheapest room total and cheapest price per guest are different
offers.

## Displaying discounts

Resolve `activePromotionIds` and each minimum's `appliedPromotionIds`
against the package's `promotions`. The first explains conditions relevant
to the period; the second identifies promotions applied to the displayed
winner. A promotion on another eligible room can split the period without
changing the lowest price.

Use the [Promotion fields](/api/objects/promotion#fields-and-night-bounds)
to explain conditions:

- Show the localized name and `percentage` with the discounted nights `from`
  and `to`. A null `to` means no end date.
- For the chosen hotel, include promotions with `appliesTo: null` or a
  matching `appliesTo[].hotelId`. The detail's `hotelId` parameter does not
  narrow `promotions`.
- Resolve `roomTypeIds` against the [hotel's room catalog](/api/objects/hotel).
  Null means all room types of that hotel. `appliesTo: null` covers all
  rooms at all hotels selling the package.

An expanded detail includes ended promotions for historical periods.
For a current promotion list, filter their dates rather than assuming every
returned promotion is still running or upcoming.

For a savings badge, use `100 × (1 − amountMinor / originalMinor)` from the
exact metric you display, before rounding prices for display. Show it only
when `original` is non-null. Label it as the saving on the displayed rate;
do not copy a promotion's percentage onto every price in its period or apply
the discount to the returned amount again.

## Opening a package from a hotel price

A hotel's `priceFrom` minima name their `visitType` and `hotel`. Take
`visitType.id` and `hotel.id` from the exact minimum you displayed (currency,
group, and metric); the two minima of one group can name different packages.
Then load:

```http
GET /api/v2/visit-types/{visitTypeId}?hotelId={hotelId}&expand=hotel,priceFromPeriods
```

Use the returned hotel listing's `priceFrom` for the complete-stay teaser and
its `priceFromPeriods` for the rate table. Use package fields for board,
treatment, and stay rules. If the detail returns `404`, the listing is
missing, `available` is false, `bookingMode` is not `instant`, or its current
`priceFrom` is null, refresh the hotel price instead of showing the outdated
amount. Period rates need not equal that teaser.
