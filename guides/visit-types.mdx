This guide walks through a package listing and a package detail with a hotel
choice and seasonal prices. Use the same [public key](/api/authentication)
for all requests of a page and request the [languages](/api/localization)
the page displays.

## Package cards

Load the [package list](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/list-visit-types) and
follow its [pagination](/api/pagination). A card typically shows the name,
`shortDescription`, the first image, the category, the board, and the stay
length from the [Visit type](/api/objects/visit-type) object. Use
`channelContent.collections` to group the cards of your own website.

For a "from" price, show `priceFrom.{currency}.perStay.amount` with the
`nights` and `occupancy` of that minimum, for example "from CZK 12,600 for
7 nights, 1 guest". Keep every displayed value from the same minimum; the
`perPersonPerNight` minimum can be a different offer. When the minimum has a
non-null `original`, show it struck through.

When `priceFrom` is `null`, show the card without a price, and label a
`request` package as available on request. The list returns only available
packages unless you set `available`; a package detail can be unavailable, so
check `available` there.

## Hotel choice

Load the [package detail](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type)
with `expand=hotels`. Each `hotels[]` entry carries the hotel summary and the
price of this package at that hotel. An entry without a buyable offer has
`priceFrom: null` but remains a hotel that sells the package. Fetch the
[Hotel](/api/objects/hotel) detail for the full profile and room catalog.

When the website visitor chooses a hotel, show that entry's price. The package's
top-level `priceFrom` compares all hotels and can belong to a different hotel
than the selected one.

## Stay rules and board

Present `stayLength`, `arrival`, and `validity` as the booking constraints,
and `board.meals` as what is included and what the guest can change. The
[stay-rule algorithm](/api/visit-types#testing-a-stay-against-its-rules)
tells you whether a chosen arrival and length is permitted. It does not tell
you whether a room is free.

## Seasonal prices

Request the detail with `expand=seasons` and `hotelId={hotelId}` to get the
seasons of the chosen hotel. Add `hotels` to the expansion when you also need
the hotel summary. Each season in `hotels[0].seasons` has the inclusive
arrival dates `from` and `to` and its own `priceFrom`. Keep seasons with
`priceFrom: null` visible as periods without an offer.

Show the same currency and metric in every row, and include the `nights` and
`occupancy` of each minimum, because rows can select different offers. A row
is the minimum across arrivals in its season, not a quote for every date.

## Regular and discounted price

A minimum's `amount` is the price after promotions. Its `original` is the
same offer before its promotions, or `null` when no promotion lowers its
rounded amount. Take both values from the same minimum: never combine the
`original` of one minimum with the `amount` of another, and never use a
package-level `original` for a seasonal row.

In the [seasonal example](/api/v2/reference/visit-types/get-a-visit-type),
the season from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027 at Hotel Pramen has a
`perStay` minimum of CZK 12,600 with an `original` of CZK 14,000. Show "from
CZK 12,600" with "CZK 14,000" struck through, for 7 nights and 1 guest. The
season from 1 April to 31 October 2027 has `original: null`, so show its
CZK 13,300 without a struck-through price.

The package's `promotions` explain which discounts exist and when; see
[Promotion](/api/objects/promotion). A promotion can be listed without
lowering a minimum, for example when it covers nights outside the cheapest
stay.

## Opening a package from a hotel price

A hotel's `priceFrom` minima name their `visitType` and `hotel`. Take
`visitType.id` and `hotel.id` from the exact minimum you displayed (currency,
group, and metric); the two minima of one group can name different packages.
Then load:

```http
GET /api/v2/visit-types/{visitTypeId}?hotelId={hotelId}&expand=hotels,seasons
```

Use the returned hotel entry for the price and the seasons, and the package
fields for board, treatment, and stay rules. If the detail returns `404`, the
entry is missing, or its price is `null`, refresh the hotel price instead of
showing the outdated amount.
