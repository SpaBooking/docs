title: "SpaPortal API"
description: "Hotel and stay-package data for spa and wellness websites."
icon: "bath"
---

SpaPortal API v2 provides hotel profiles, room types, amenities and
indicative prices for spa and wellness websites. Responses include localized
content and structured attributes for property listings and detail pages.

The [visit type draft](/api/visit-types) adds stay packages with meals,
treatment, stay rules, hotel offers and seasonal prices. It is available
for integration review. [API coverage](/api/versioning#api-coverage) lists
the released resources and draft additions.
