SpaPortal is a booking management system that enables you to manage reservations across multiple distribution channels from a single platform.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 05 19 At 9 47 02](/images/Screenshot-2026-05-19-at-9.47.02.png)
</Frame>

SpaPortal currently supports two channels: your own **direct channel** and the **SpaBooking partner platform**. All reservations arriving through either channel are consolidated into a unified view of availability, pricing, and reservations across all your properties.

The **SpaPortal API** extends this capability to developers, allowing you to build **custom integrations and customer-facing applications** on top of your own data. You can query your property portfolio, retrieve real-time availability and pricing, and create or manage reservations programmatically — giving you full flexibility to embed SpaPortal's functionality into your own website, mobile app, or third-party system.

## Why use SpaPortal

SpaPortal makes it easy to establish and grow your spa property's online presence.

**Centralized reservations**\
Bookings from all channels land in one place, eliminating double bookings and manual work.

**Real-time data**\
Availability and pricing always reflect the current state across all properties.

**Full flexibility**\
Build exactly the website you need, unconstrained by off-the-shelf solutions.

**Single source of truth**\
Your inventory and reservations are consistently accessible across every integration.

## What you can build with SpaPortal

**Direct booking website**\
Display your properties with live availability and pricing, and let guests book without leaving your site.

**Mobile application**\
Offer a native booking experience on iOS or Android.

**Booking widget**\
Embed a booking form or availability checker into any existing website.

**Internal tools**\
Build custom dashboards or back-office views tailored to your team's needs.
