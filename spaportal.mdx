title: "SpaPortal API"
description: "Hotel and stay-package data for spa and wellness websites."
icon: "bath"
---

SpaPortal API v2 provides hotel profiles, room types, amenities, stay
packages, and indicative prices for spa and wellness websites. Responses
include localized content and structured attributes for listings and detail
pages.

[Visit types](/api/visit-types) describe the stay packages with their meals,
treatment, stay rules, hotel offers, and seasonal prices.
